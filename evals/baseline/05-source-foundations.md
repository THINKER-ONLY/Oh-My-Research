## Raw Output

Citation and evidence integrity are hard gates because they govern a manuscript’s truth conditions. The Skill requires each substantive claim to match verified evidence, scope, qualifiers, numbers, and an exact source location; unsupported material must remain a visible gap rather than being repaired with plausible prose or citations (`SKILL.md`, “Non-negotiable rules”; `references/evidence-and-integrity.md`, “Claim Test” and “Citations”; `references/writing-workflow.md`, “Ledger” and “Audit”). These constraints prevent fabrication, evidence drift, and unsupported causal or general claims.

Voice, section order, and information structure are adaptable because they are rhetorical means rather than evidence. They may change with audience, genre, section purpose, and task depth, provided revision preserves proposition, scope, polarity, qualifiers, numbers, and material limitations (`references/revision-and-style.md`, “Two-Pass Revision”; `references/section-guides.md`, “Paragraph Check”). The bundle itself reflects this distinction by prescribing invariant integrity checks while directing writers to load only relevant guidance and use the smallest workflow that protects the claim.
