Before drafting, convert every substantive planned claim into an evidence-ledger row. A claim with a `GAP` is a question or placeholder, not a sentence to make more persuasive. Preserve the supplied original and do not silently alter it; record substantive changes for author approval. In the ledger copy, remove or replace the instructional `TEMPLATE-01` row before adding claims—never treat that row as a claim or deliver it.
